## Brand Context Profile

Status: drafted 2026-09-20 from live store signals (shop description, collection and page copy, product catalog). PENDING owner confirmation before it drives any writing. This brand is separate from the other stores on the account. Do not import their voice, keywords or audience.

- **Positioning (one line):** Canada's dedicated adventure motorcycle camping gear shop. Curated, field-tested moto camping kit and mapped routes for riders who go past the pavement.
- **What it sells:** Ultra-lightweight adventure moto gear, dual-sport soft luggage, Titan Straps (12 sizes), camp kitchen and stoves (46 items), apparel, satellite communicators and rider comms (ZOLEO), digital GPX route packs and online courses, and guided events and tours. 155 products in total.
- **Primary customer:** Adventure and dual-sport motorcycle riders who camp off the bike. They ride the backcountry, want gear that survives rough roads, and value lightweight, proven kit over showroom gear.
- **Secondary customer:** Newer ADV riders kitting out their first backcountry trip, and riders booking guided tours or buying route guides.
- **Customer vocabulary (their words, not ours):** ADV, adventure motorcycle, dual-sport, moto camping, backcountry, soft luggage, panniers, Titan Straps, camp stove, satellite communicator, GPX tracks, TCAT, Trans Canada Adventure Trail.
- **Geography and market:** Canada. Strong focus on BC, Alberta, the Canadian Rockies and the Kootenays. Currency CAD. Routes and events are Canada based.
- **Seasonality (peaks, lulls, buying calendar, which products each season lifts):** Riding season runs roughly May to October and drives gear, luggage, camp kitchen and comms demand. Winter shifts to planning and research, gift buying, and digital downloads like GPX packs and courses. Guided events and tours cluster in summer. Prepare peak-season gear terms in late winter, ahead of the spring buy.
- **Tone: do:** Confident and plain-spoken. First person "we". Dry humor. Practical and field-tested. Community and stewardship minded.
  - Example line 1: "Adventure motorcycle travel and moto camping done properly."
  - Example line 2: "If you treat a wrong turn as part of the plan, you are in the right place."
  - Example line 3: "Comfortable, durable layers that look as good at the brewery as they do at a backcountry camp."
- **Tone: do not:** No corporate filler. No breathless hype or superlatives the gear cannot back. No generic outdoor cliches. Do not sound like a big-box retailer. Do not oversell. Let the field testing speak.
- **Competitors (research only, never named in copy):** Other ADV moto gear specialists such as Mosko Moto, Kriega, Giant Loop and Adventure Spec. Used to find keyword gaps only.
- **Sibling brand firewall:** This brand is I Think We Missed a Turn. It is not Poet Snow Sports or Fly Guys. Keep them fully separate. No snow sports language, no other brand's voice or keywords.
