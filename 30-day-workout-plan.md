# 🏆 30-Day Movement Challenge

**Goals:** lose weight · tone up · strengthen core · ease shoulder & neck pain · cut tension headaches

**Plan:** 30 days straight, **6 days a week**, rotating:
🏃 3–5 km run · 🚴 bike ride · 🚲 30-min Peloton (strength/core) · 🧘‍♀️ Pilates · 🔥 hot yoga

**Dates:** Sat Jul 18 → Sun Aug 16, 2026

---

## How this plan targets your goals
| Your goal | How the plan gets you there |
|---|---|
| **Lose weight** | Frequent cardio (run · bike · Peloton rides) + daily movement keeps the burn consistent — the real driver is showing up 6×/week. |
| **Tone up** | Peloton is programmed as **strength classes** (arms, core, full body), backed by Pilates. |
| **Strengthen core** | Pilates 5× + core-focused Peloton — the foundation for everything else. |
| **Ease shoulder & neck pain** | Hot yoga 5× with neck/shoulder openers + Pilates postural work to un-round the shoulders. |
| **Fewer tension headaches** | A short **daily Tension Reset** (below) fights the inactivity + tension that trigger them. |

---

## 🔄 Daily Tension Reset — do this *every* day (even rest days)
3–5 minutes, targets the neck/shoulder tightness behind the headaches:
1. **Chin tucks** ×10 — gently draw chin back, lengthen the back of the neck
2. **Shoulder rolls** ×10 back, ×10 forward
3. **Ear-to-shoulder neck stretch** — 30 s each side
4. **Doorway chest stretch** — 30 s (opens the front, un-rounds shoulders)
5. **Upper-trap release** — 30 s each side
> Also: stand and move for 2 min every hour you're sitting. Movement snacks > one big fix.

---

## How it works
- **6 active days + 1 rest day** each week — rest on **Sundays**.
- Runs progress 3 km → 5 km; no two runs back-to-back.
- Hot yoga & Pilates fall after harder days for recovery and posture reset.
- Check the box each day. **Streak > perfection** — miss one, don't reset, just continue.

**Legend:** 🏃 Run · 🚴 Bike · 🚲 Peloton · 🧘‍♀️ Pilates · 🔥 Hot yoga · 😴 Rest
